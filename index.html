About LPU HostelCare

LPU HostelCare is a student-focused hostel maintenance and management platform concept designed for Lovely Professional University. It aims to simplify communication between hostel residents and maintenance administrators through a centralized digital complaint management system.

The platform allows students to report maintenance issues while helping administrators organize, review, and manage requests efficiently.

ⓘ This is an independent academic frontend concept. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or integrated with Lovely Professional University.
